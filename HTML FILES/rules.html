Academic Rules & Regulations

Code of Conduct

Strictly to be followed by all students.

  • 1. Punctuality is Mandatory

    Students must enter the school premises strictly within 15 minutes of the bell. Latecomers will face strict disciplinary action and may be sent back home.

  • 2. Strict Dress Code

    Students must wear proper, clean, and neatly ironed school uniform. Nails must be trimmed, and hair should be properly combed. No fancy accessories are allowed.

  • 3. Identity Cards

    It is mandatory for all students to wear their school ID cards every day. Entry without an ID card is strictly prohibited.

  • 4. Prohibited Items

    Mobile phones, smartwatches, expensive gadgets, and junk food are strictly not allowed inside the school campus.

  • 5. Behavior & Discipline

    Respectful behavior towards teachers, staff, and fellow students is expected at all times. Any form of bullying or misbehavior will not be tolerated.
